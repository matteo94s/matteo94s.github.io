Short Bio

I am a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering with experience across biomedical signal processing, algorithm development, large scale multimodal neural and cardiovascular data analysis, and Machine Learning (ML). Industry experience in R&D with exposure to cardiac electrophysiology, and medical device software development. My profile incorporates scientific research, clinical translation, and medical device R&D. As a person I am a very outgoing, I love challenges, and I am always eager to learn more.

Research interests

All research areas and projects

Brain-Heart Interplay in Neurology

Estimation of directed interactions between cortical and cardiac signals.

Home-based stroke rehabilitation

Development of medical devices to monitor and improve the recovery of stroke patients at home.

AI in sleep and cardiology

Development of AI-driven solutions for sleep and cardiology research.

Current directions

  • Deep Multiview Representation Learning in signal processing
  • Causal discovery and explainable AI for Brain-Heart Interplay in Epilepsy
