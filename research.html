Research

My strongest obsession lately has been to derive a time-varying, directed, and complex representation of the interactions between cortical and cardiovascular signals using deep multiview representation learning. Other research interests can be found in the areas below.

Current directions

  • Deep Multiview Representation Learning in signal processing
  • Causal discovery and explainable AI for Brain-Heart Interplay in Epilepsy
