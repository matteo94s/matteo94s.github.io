Directional Brain-Heart Interaction During Cognitive Workload: A Permutation Conditional Mutual Information Approach

Matteo Saibene, Martin Ballegaard, Tobias Andersen, Ying Gu, Jakob E. Bardram, Sadasivan Puthusserypady

Journal article Open accessIn print

Abstract

This study investigates Permutation Conditional Mutual Information (PCMI) for measuring directional brain-heart interactions during cognitive workload. The approach was evaluated using synthetic coupled time series and simultaneous EEG and ECG recordings from 36 participants during baseline and mental arithmetic tasks. Results indicate increased brain-heart interaction complexity during cognitive workload, particularly across anterior and posterior scalp regions and autonomic cardiovascular components. The findings highlight PCMI as a promising method for investigating nonlinear directional interactions between the brain and heart.
