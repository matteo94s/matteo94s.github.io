PySDG - a Python-based Framework for Time-Varying Directional Brain-Heart Interplay Measurement through Synthetic Data Generation Models

Alexander Borch Kristensen, Matteo Saibene, Vincenzo Catrambone, Nicolai Spicher, Sadasivan Puthusserypady

Journal article Open access

Abstract

Brain-Heart Interaction (BHI) characterizes the bidirectional communication between the central nervous system and autonomic nervous system. The Synthetic Data Generation model (SDG) is an established tool to quantify time-varying and directional BHI based on electroencephalographic and electrocardiographic data. In this work, we present pySDG, a novel and easy-to-use python implementation of the Synthetic Data Generation (SDG) model, which eliminates the need for proprietary licenses and achieves a significant reduction in computational time compared to its MATLAB equivalent. Thereby, pySDG enables BHI quantification in long time measurements and through its simple interface we aim to make the analysis of BHI more accessible to other researchers.
