Privacy notice

This site is built to collect as little as possible.

Who is responsible

This website is operated by Matteo Saibene. Questions about this notice can be sent to saima@dtu.dk.

What the site stores in your browser

  • Your light or dark mode choice, saved in your browser’s local storage under the key theme. It never leaves your device.
  • No cookies are set by this site.

Analytics

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts on this site.

Third parties

  • Fonts and icons are served from this site, not from a font provider.
  • Google Maps loads only if you press “Load map” on a page that offers it. Doing so sends your IP address and browser details to Google. You can use the plain “Open in Google Maps” link instead.
  • Links to other sites (publishers, Google Scholar, ORCID, code hosting, social networks) are governed by those sites’ own policies.

Contact form

The contact form does not send data anywhere. It opens your own email program with the message filled in, and you decide whether to send it.

Server logs

The hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address, requested page and time) for security and operations. This site’s owner does not use them to identify visitors.
