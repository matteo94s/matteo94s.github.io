AI and crawler policy

Last reviewed 16 October 2026.

AI assistance on this site

This website is written and maintained by Matteo Saibene.

AI tools may have been used for editing, coding assistance, or drafting. Matteo remains responsible for the accuracy and final content.

Individual blog posts disclose material AI assistance where applicable.

Individual blog posts can carry their own disclosure. A post without one makes no claim either way.

Use of this site’s content by automated systems

Training
Use of this website's content for training, fine-tuning, evaluating, or improving language models is not permitted without prior written permission.
Retrieval
Search and retrieval use is welcome when crawlers respect robots.txt and do not overload the server.
Citation
Short quotations are welcome when linked to the original page.

What these files can and cannot do

  • robots.txt is a request that well-behaved crawlers choose to honour. It does not technically prevent access, and some operators ignore it.
  • llms.txt and llms-full.txt are plain-text summaries of this site written for language models. llms.txt is a community proposal, not an established standard, and no one is obliged to read it.
  • Crawler permissions are separate from copyright. Permitting a crawler does not grant a licence, and disallowing one does not change the legal status of the content.
